# Vitala Support

Thanks for using Vitala. Here are answers to the most common questions.

## Do I need an account?

No. Vitala keeps everything on your iPhone. There's nothing to sign up for.

## Can I use Vitala on more than one iPhone?

Yes. Tap your avatar, then **Settings › iCloud sync**, and turn on **Sync with iCloud** on each iPhone. Your logs, journal, favourite foods, saved meals and the workouts you build then match on every device signed in to the same Apple Account. They are kept in your own private iCloud, which only you can read. Vitala syncs when you open it and a few seconds after you log something. Your profile, goals and photo stay on each iPhone, so you set those up once per device.

If the screen says **Not signed in to iCloud**, sign in from the iOS Settings app and come back.

## How do I connect Apple Health?

Open **Today**, tap your avatar, then **Apple Health › Connect Apple Health**. To change what Vitala can read or write later, go to **Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Vitala** on your iPhone.

## Why don't I see steps or heart rate?

These come from Apple Health. Make sure Health is connected and that Vitala has permission to read those types. Some data (like heart rate) needs an Apple Watch.

## How do reminders work?

Tap your avatar, then **Settings › Reminders**. Turn each reminder on or off and choose its time. If notifications are off for Vitala, the screen links you to iOS Settings.

## The breathing sessions play sound — can I change it?

Yes. During a session, tap the speaker button to choose **Spa, binaural & bowls**, **Spa & binaural**, **Spa**, **Crystal bowls**, **Ocean** or **Silent**. Binaural tones work best with headphones. Each session starts on Spa, binaural & bowls.

## Does a session keep going if I lock my iPhone?

Yes, as long as a sound is playing. Lock the screen and the sound carries on, the session keeps time, and it is saved when it ends. A paused session, or one set to **Silent**, waits for you instead: its clock stops while the screen is off.

## Can I change the background or turn off the vibrations?

Yes. The picture button beside the speaker chooses the scene; each session opens on the one that matches it. The speaker menu also has a **Breathing haptics** switch. With **Reduce Motion** on in iOS Settings, scenes hold still.

## Where does food nutrition come from?

Food search uses USDA FoodData Central, a free public nutrition database from the U.S. Department of Agriculture. Only the words you type are sent. Foods you add are saved on your iPhone so they're searchable offline. To keep search fully offline, turn off **Settings › Food search**.

## Can I log food from restaurants?

Yes. In **Log a meal**, type a chain's name or tap it in the row under the search field to see its menu, then choose an item. Vitala lists the chains USDA FoodData Central has analysed, including McDonald's, Wendy's, Burger King, Taco Bell, KFC, Subway and Pizza Hut. USDA doesn't cover every chain, and menus change, so check the restaurant's own figures when they matter. If something is missing, add it as a custom food.

## How do I save foods and meals I eat often?

In **Log a meal**, tap the star beside any food to make it a favourite; favourites are listed first. To keep a whole meal, add its foods, tap **Save as a usual meal** and give it a name: it appears under **Your meals** and adds everything in one tap. An empty meal also offers **Same as yesterday** when you logged that meal the day before. Touch and hold a saved meal to delete it.

## Can I make my own workout?

Yes. In **Activity**, tap **Build your own workout**. Give it a name, tap **Add exercises** and pick as many as you like (the same one more than once if you want), then tap an exercise to set its time or its reps and sets. Drag to reorder with **Edit**, swipe to remove. Your workout appears above the library marked **Yours**; open it and tap **Edit** to change or delete it.

## Where can I see how my mood has changed?

The **Journal** section in **Mindful** shows the last seven days' moods; tap a day to see its entries. For the longer view, tap your avatar, then **Progress**, and scroll to **Mood**: each day's mood from low to great over 7 or 30 days, and the moods you chose most often.

## How do I see how an exercise is done?

Open a workout and tap any exercise for a looping demonstration with instructions. The same demonstration plays during the workout and pauses when you do. With **Reduce Motion** on in iOS Settings, it shows the key position without moving.

## How do I scan a barcode?

In **Log a meal**, tap **Scan barcode** and point the camera at the barcode on the package. You can also type the number printed under the barcode. Vitala looks it up in USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts; if a product isn't found, you can add it as a custom food.

## How do I back up or move my data?

Tap your avatar, then **Settings › Export my data**. Vitala creates a JSON file you can save to Files or share.

## How do I delete my data?

Tap your avatar, then **Settings › Erase all data**. This permanently removes everything Vitala stored on your device. With iCloud sync on, it also removes your logs from iCloud and from your other devices that sync. Data already saved to Apple Health stays in Health.

To remove only the copy in iCloud and keep what is on your iPhone, use **Settings › iCloud sync › Delete the iCloud copy**.

## Vitala said my data file was damaged. What happened?

Vitala found it couldn't read its data file when it opened. It set the file aside rather than deleting it, and carried on from the copy it saved the last time it opened cleanly, so anything logged since then may be missing. If only a few entries were damaged, it kept everything else. The file it set aside can be sent to support, or deleted, from **Settings › Recovered data**.

## Is Vitala medical advice?

No. Vitala is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Talk to a healthcare professional about medical questions.
