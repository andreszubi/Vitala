# Vitala Support

Thanks for using Vitala. Here are answers to the most common questions.

## Do I need an account?

No. Vitala keeps everything on your iPhone. There's nothing to sign up for.

## How do I connect Apple Health?

Open **Today**, tap your avatar, then **Apple Health › Connect Apple Health**. To change what Vitala can read or write later, go to **Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Vitala** on your iPhone.

## Why don't I see steps or heart rate?

These come from Apple Health. Make sure Health is connected and that Vitala has permission to read those types. Some data (like heart rate) needs an Apple Watch.

## How do reminders work?

Tap your avatar, then **Settings › Reminders**. Turn each reminder on or off and choose its time. If notifications are off for Vitala, the screen links you to iOS Settings.

## The breathing sessions play sound — can I change it?

Yes. During a session, tap the speaker button to choose **Spa & binaural**, **Spa**, **Ocean** or **Silent**. Binaural tones work best with headphones. Your choice is remembered.

## How do I back up or move my data?

Tap your avatar, then **Settings › Export my data**. Vitala creates a JSON file you can save to Files or share.

## How do I delete my data?

Tap your avatar, then **Settings › Erase all data**. This permanently removes everything Vitala stored on your device. Data already saved to Apple Health stays in Health.

## Is Vitala medical advice?

No. Vitala is a wellness companion, not a medical device. Talk to a healthcare professional about medical questions.
