# Vitala Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Vitala is built so your wellness data stays yours.

## What Vitala stores

Everything you enter — your name, an optional profile photo, birthday, height, weight and goals, and the water, meals, workouts, sleep, mindful sessions and journal entries you log — is stored **on your iPhone**, in Vitala's private app storage, protected by iOS Data Protection. Unless you turn on iCloud sync, it stays only there. This includes the weights you record and any GLP-1 doses and side effects you log.

Vitala has no servers of its own for your data. We never receive, sell or share what you log.

## Your account

Vitala needs an account. You can create one with **Sign in with Apple**, with **Google**, or with an **email address and a password**.

Accounts are kept by [Firebase Authentication](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), a service of Google. It stores:

- your email address, or the private relay address Apple gives you if you choose to hide it;
- the name Apple or Google shares, if any;
- an account identifier;
- for email accounts, a protected form of your password that cannot be read back.

**Nothing you log in Vitala is stored with your account**: no meals, workouts, sleep, weight, medicines, side effects, journal entries or Apple Health data. The account is used only to sign you in. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

You can delete your account at any time in **Settings › Account › Delete account**. That removes the account from Firebase and erases your data from your iPhone.

## iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on **Settings › iCloud sync**, Vitala keeps a copy of your logs (including weights and GLP-1 doses and side effects), journal entries, favourite foods, saved meals and the workouts you build in **your own private iCloud storage**, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account show the same data.

- Apple stores it under your account and encrypts it in transit and on its servers. Vitala's developer cannot read it.
- Your profile, goals and photo are not synced.
- Nothing Vitala reads from Apple Health is ever included.
- You can turn sync off at any time. **Settings › iCloud sync › Delete the iCloud copy** removes the copy from iCloud.

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Vitala:

- **reads** steps, active energy, exercise minutes, walking and running distance, heart rate, sleep and mindful minutes, plus the food energy, protein, carbs and fat that other apps record, to show your progress;
- **writes** the water, sleep, mindful sessions and workouts you log in Vitala.

Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never leaves your device through Vitala. You can change or revoke access at any time in **Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Vitala**.

## Food search and barcodes

When you search for a food, Vitala sends **only the words you type** (for example, "banana") to [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov), a public U.S. government nutrition database.

When you scan or type a barcode, Vitala sends **only the barcode number** to USDA FoodData Central and, if the product isn't found there, to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database.

No name, account, device identifier or health data is sent with either. Foods you add are saved on your device so you can find them again offline. You can turn online lookups off in **Settings › Food search**; searches then only cover foods saved on your device and scanning is hidden.

## Camera

Vitala uses the camera only to read barcodes while the scanner is open. Nothing is recorded or saved.

## Photos

If you add a profile photo, you choose it with the system photo picker. Vitala only receives the single photo you select, never your library.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Analytics and tracking

Vitala contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Your choices

- **Export:** Settings › Export my data creates a JSON file of everything Vitala stores, which you can save or share.
- **Erase:** Settings › Erase all data permanently removes everything Vitala stored on your device. With iCloud sync on, it also removes your logs from iCloud and from your other devices that sync. Data already saved to Apple Health stays in Health, where you can delete it.
- **Delete your account:** Settings › Account › Delete account removes your Vitala account and erases your data from your device.
- **Delete the app:** removes Vitala's data from your device, but not your account. Delete the account first if you want it gone.

## Children

Vitala is not directed to children under 13.
