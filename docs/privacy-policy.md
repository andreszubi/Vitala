# Vitala Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Vitala is built so your wellness data stays yours.

## What Vitala stores

Everything you enter — your name, an optional profile photo, birthday, height, weight and goals, and the water, meals, workouts, sleep and mindful sessions you log — is stored **only on your iPhone**, in Vitala's private app storage, protected by iOS Data Protection.

Vitala has no account system and no servers. We never receive, sell or share your data.

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Vitala:

- **reads** steps, active energy, exercise minutes, walking and running distance, heart rate, sleep and mindful minutes to show your daily progress;
- **writes** the water, sleep, mindful sessions and workouts you log in Vitala.

Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never leaves your device through Vitala. You can change or revoke access at any time in **Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Vitala**.

## Photos

If you add a profile photo, you choose it with the system photo picker. Vitala only receives the single photo you select, never your library.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Analytics and tracking

Vitala contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Your choices

- **Export:** Settings › Export my data creates a JSON file of everything Vitala stores, which you can save or share.
- **Erase:** Settings › Erase all data permanently removes everything Vitala stored on your device. Data already saved to Apple Health stays in Health, where you can delete it.
- **Delete the app:** removes all of Vitala's data from your device.

## Children

Vitala is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new date at the top.
